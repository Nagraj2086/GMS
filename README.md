# GMS
Gym Management System

## Welcome to RD Gym

**RD Gym** is a modern Gym Management System designed to make gym management simple, organized, and efficient for both administrators and members.

Our system provides a centralized platform to manage **members, membership plans, trainers, payments, workout plans, attendance, and feedback**.

### What We Provide

**Member Management**
Administrators can maintain member details, membership information, assigned trainers, and account-related information in one place.

**Membership Management**
Create and manage different membership plans with duration, fees, and plan details. Members can easily view their current membership information.

**Payment Management**
Record and track member payments, payment status, payment modes, and remaining membership amounts.

**Workout Plans**
Trainers or administrators can create personalized workout plans for members, including workout titles and descriptions.

**Trainer Management**
Manage trainer information and assign trainers to members according to their requirements.

**Attendance Tracking**
Maintain member attendance records and help administrators monitor gym participation.

**Member Feedback**
Members can submit feedback, while administrators can review feedback submitted by gym members.

## Our Goal

The goal of RD Gym is to reduce manual work and provide an organized digital solution for managing day-to-day gym activities.

By bringing important gym operations into a single system, RD Gym helps administrators manage information efficiently while giving members easy access to their membership and workout-related details.

## For Members

Members can:

* View their membership details
* Check payment information
* View assigned workout plans
* Check trainer details
* Track attendance
* Submit feedback

## For Administrators

Administrators can:

* Manage members
* Create membership plans
* Manage trainers
* Record and track payments
* Assign workout plans
* Monitor attendance
* Review member feedback
